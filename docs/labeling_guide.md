# Role labeling guide

Label one canonical vacancy, never an individual snapshot. Use title, description and skills; do
not infer a role from the employer. Record label, annotator, label version, reason and date in the
production labeling table. Double-label at least 10% of future data and resolve disagreements.

Initial labels are `data_analyst`, `product_analyst`, `bi_analyst`, `system_analyst`,
`financial_analyst`, `data_scientist`, `other_analytics`, `non_target`, and `unclear`.

The committed CSV is a synthetic smoke dataset only. It must not be used to claim market metrics.
